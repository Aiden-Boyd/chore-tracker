# Chore Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Chore Tracker collects the email address used to sign in. It is used only to authenticate the account and deliver one-time sign-in codes. The authentication service stores account and session records, and Resend processes the email delivery.

Names, chores, rewards, payment history, and notification preferences are stored locally on the user’s device for the signed-in account. Chore Tracker 1.0 does not upload that household data to the authentication service and does not provide cross-device household sharing.

The app does not sell personal data, use third-party advertising, or track users across apps or websites. Local notifications are scheduled on the device and can be disabled in the app or iOS Settings.

Users can delete their account from Profile → Delete Account. This deletes the authentication account and removes that account’s local household data from the device. Users can also contact New Life Media for privacy or support requests.
