# Chore Tracker Support

For help with sign-in, chores, rewards, payments, or account deletion, email support@newlifemedia.co.

Include the app version, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send sign-in codes or other sensitive information.

Chore Tracker 1.0 stores household data on the device. Deleting the app may remove local chores and payment history, so confirm important records before uninstalling.
